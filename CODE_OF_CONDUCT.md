# Code of conduct

Help keep this small project a respectful place to collaborate.

- Be kind, constructive and specific when discussing changes.
- Respect Serene's identity, personal content and decisions about her portfolio.
- Welcome contributors regardless of background or experience.
- Respect privacy; do not share private contact details, credentials or personal information in issues or pull requests.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others or post spam.

Focus feedback on the work rather than the person. Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to **[mensahserene2020@gmail.com](mailto:mensahserene2020@gmail.com)**. Share only the details necessary to explain the concern. Reports will be handled with discretion, subject to what is necessary to review and address them.
